Stone from survey to slab

Surveying, drilling and excavation that turn a hillside into dimension stone, with processing units at Tagena in Bhutan and Rajsamand in Rajasthan.

The Bhutan mine is run by our subsidiary, Karma CP Systems Private Limited, and holds one of the largest white granite reserves in the region.

The two mines

Bhutan Granite Mine, Wangdue

About 85 acres, 8 million MT of reserves, CP-DRUK White Galaxy.

Kekri Granite Mine, Rajasthan

One hectare, about 1 million MT: Steel Grey, Black Galaxy, Red Galaxy.
