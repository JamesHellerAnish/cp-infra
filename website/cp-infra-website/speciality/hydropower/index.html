Hydropower and river dredging

Dams, tunnels and rivers that cannot be switched off: rehabilitation, dredging, grouting and repair done inside shutdown windows.

Scroll to the work

Work on a running river

Dam and tunnel rehabilitation, dredging, baffle walls, drilling and grouting, shotcreting, slope stabilisation, anchoring, micro piling and chemical treatments.

CPS crews work with water still flowing, in extreme weather and under strict safety rules, and to the shutdown dates set by the plant.
