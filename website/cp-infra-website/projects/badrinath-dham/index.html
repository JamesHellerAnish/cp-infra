A riverfront for the yatra

The redevelopment of Shri Badrinath Dham runs along both banks of the Alaknanda. CPS built three riverfronts, the pedestrian bridge between them and a civic centre near Badrish Lake.

Every part of it had to be founded below a fast, freezing river, and finished between yatra crowds.

What the site asked of us

−10 to 20 °C

Below zero from October to April. Concrete takes longer to set even with admixtures, so pours are planned around the cold.

5–7 m below the bed

Foundations in bouldery strata with seepage that 10–12 dewatering pumps could not fully stop. Some days the only way across was the bucket.

May – November yatra

Pilgrim traffic restricts equipment movement and slows material supply, with homes on both banks. Work carries on after dark.

Footbridge and civic centre

47 m spanNear Badrish Lake

A 47 m pedestrian bridge across the Alaknanda, and a Civic Centre building near Badrish Lake. The bridge piers sit 5–7 m below the riverbed, so seepage control set the pace.

  • Bridge foundations 5–7 m below the riverbed
  • Raft and column reinforcement for the civic centre
  • Concrete pumped across the site
  • Structure carried on through winter snow
