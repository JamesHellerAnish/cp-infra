Bhutan Granite Mine

Eighty-five acres of white granite above the Wangdue valley, quarried by wire saw and finished at our own processing unit.

From the bench to the slab

The mine is run by Karma CP Systems Private Limited, a CP Systems subsidiary, and produces CP-DRUK White Galaxy for flooring, countertops and facades.

Blocks are freed from the face with diamond wire saws, squared on the bench, stacked for dispatch and cut into slabs on gang saws at Tagena.

A day on the quarry

The Tagena processing unit

Five acres in a forested fold of the valley, with a gantry yard for blocks, a cutting shed and stock of finished White Galaxy slabs.
