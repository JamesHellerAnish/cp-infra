Hailang Dam

Rebuilding the gate grooves of a diversion tunnel at the 444 MW Vishnugad Pipalkoti project, with the river still running through it.

Two gates, one tunnel, no shutdown

Two gates sit in the 10.5 × 10.5 m box section of the diversion tunnel, about 16 m from the portal and 50 m from the intake. Their grooves, pier and side walls were badly damaged.

CPS repaired them one side at a time across two lean seasons: the right gate in 2024, the left in 2025. The tunnel kept carrying 50–60 cumec the whole time.

How the repair was done

Twelve steps, the same on each side, from cutting a way in through the tunnel roof to closing it again with a steel plate.

  1. Debris removed from the top slab of the DT
  2. DT top slab cut open with a diamond wire saw
  3. Coffer dam of boulder-filled MS boxes in 50–60 cumec of flow
  4. MS boxes lowered into the flowing river by crane
  5. Loose concrete scaled off with breakers
  6. New reinforcement drilled, anchored and fixed
  7. Micro concrete poured above and under water
  8. Cracks and honeycombing sealed with epoxy putty
  9. Concrete surfaces repaired with epoxy mortar
  10. Three-component epoxy grout in the groove and T guide plate
  11. Boulder-filled MS boxes removed
  12. MS cover plate fabricated and fixed over the cut

Phase 1: right bay and gate groove

Jan – Mar 2024Lean season

The right gate came first: open the slab, push the water to one side of the tunnel, and rebuild the pier and walls in the dry half.

  • DT slab cut for access
  • Temporary diversion made inside the tunnel
  • Pier and side walls rebuilt
  • Concrete placed through hopper and pipe
