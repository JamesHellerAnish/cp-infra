Tapovan Vishnugad HPP

RC dam repairRebaring and micro concrete

Repair and structural strengthening of the dam's reinforced concrete: damaged concrete cut out, new steel coupled in, and the section rebuilt in micro concrete and epoxy mortar.

  • Loose concrete removed
  • Rebaring with couplers and shear connectors
  • High-strength micro concrete
  • Epoxy mortar finish

Maneri Bhali Stage-I Dam

Spillway and roller bucketLean season, under water

Damaged seal beams, heavy silt erosion and deep cavities in the spillway and roller bucket, repaired under water in the lean season.

  • Profile matching in M80 concrete
  • Rebaring and epoxy grouting
  • MS plate liners for spillway and roller buckets
  • Seal beams and silt-eroded cavities rebuilt

Koteshwar Hydroelectric Project

36 monthsLeft bank, up to 200 m

Stabilisation of the left bank up to 200 m above the riverbed, then grouting and epoxy treatment at the power intake and turbine bases.

  • 20,000+ sqm secured with rock bolts and chain link
  • Shotcreting of the slope
  • Epoxy and PU grouting at the power intake
  • Turbine base plates and construction joints treated

VPHEP Pipalkoti: freeing the TBM

From 20211.5 years

In 2021 the tunnel boring machine at VPHEP stuck fast in a fragile section of the HRT. Over a year and a half CPS drilled and grouted the sandy ground around it until the TBM could move again.

  • Consolidation drilling in fragile, sandy strata
  • Polyurethane grouting
  • Microfine cement grouting
  • Colloidal silica injection

Sewa II HEP, Jammu & Kashmir

201010 km, 3.3 m diameter

Repair of a 10 km head race tunnel only 3.3 m across, with severe leakage. Machinery went in by hand through the few entry points.

  • Heavy machinery carried in by hand from few entry points
  • PU grouting to stop severe leakage
  • Epoxy mortar repair of the HRT
