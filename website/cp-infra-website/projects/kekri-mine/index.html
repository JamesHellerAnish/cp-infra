A small mine, three stones

Kekri produces Steel Grey, Black Galaxy and Red Galaxy granite from about 1 million MT of reserves.

Blocks go to the CPS processing unit at Rajsamand near Udaipur, where they are cut and finished for flooring, countertops and facades.
