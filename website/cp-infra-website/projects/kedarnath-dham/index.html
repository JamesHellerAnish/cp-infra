Rooms for pilgrims at 3,583 m

Blocks Q2Q and Q1N give pilgrims somewhere warm to sleep at one of India's most sacred sites.

Every bag of cement and length of steel came up the valley; altitude and weather made the programme short and the work technically demanding. CPS did all of it, structure to finish.

  • Excavation
  • Concreting
  • Cutting, binding and fixing reinforcement
  • Formwork
  • Structural steel trusses
  • False ceilings
  • Tile, Kota stone and granite
  • Doors, windows and ventilators
  • Stone cladding
  • Plumbing
  • Electrical work
  • Painting and finishing
