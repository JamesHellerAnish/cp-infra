How we build

CPS was founded on innovation, ingenuity and purpose, and on a firm belief in meaningful, timely execution.

We are judged by what we build and by how we build it: with integrity, precision and a forward-thinking mind. Alongside execution, CPS has grown into civil and structural design and into turnkey project delivery, end to end.

Our clients are government and semi-government departments, public sector undertakings, turnkey operators, promoters and builders.

Led from the site

Under Sanjay Shandilya, CMD, CP Systems has grown from a specialist contractor into a multi-disciplinary infrastructure company working across India and in Bhutan.

The team covers civil, anti-corrosive, mechanical and electrical execution. Many of our people came from leading infrastructure companies and helped shape the country's infrastructure map before joining CPS.

Udyog Ratan and Excellence Awards

Recognised nationally in 2000–01 with the Udyog Ratan Award and the Excellence Award.

Our aim is to reach the top of the industry by delivering exceptional value: new markets, strong partnerships, a skilled team and quality clients can rely on.

Growth, year by year

Turnover rose 154.7% in FY 2023–24 and another 130.9% in FY 2024–25, driven by new markets, partnerships, skilled teams and reliable quality.
