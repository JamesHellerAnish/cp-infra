Hard ground
since 1999

Established in 1999, CPS works where timing is tight and access is worse: under running rivers, inside tunnels, at 3,000 metres and in the quarry.

Our clients are government departments, public sector undertakings such as THDC India, turnkey operators and builders. The work spans civil, anti-corrosive, mechanical and electrical execution, and a growing practice in civil and structural design.

Between windows

No shutdown on record this month. Crews mobilise, plan the next window and keep the year-round work going: Badrinath runs April to December each year, and the mines in Bhutan and Rajasthan keep cutting.
